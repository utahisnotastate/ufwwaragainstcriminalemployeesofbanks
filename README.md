# War Against Criminal Employees of Banks

**An open catalogue of 100 banking-crime hypotheses, published for public review.** Anyone can read the documents, check the claims, and challenge the ratings.

The catalogue backs an [open petition](petition/the-case-for-nobel-recognition.md) asking the Nobel institutions to recognize the fight against crime in the banking industry. No Nobel Prize in Warfare exists, and the petition says so.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **These documents are leads, not evidence.** They describe theoretical schemes and name no suspect. Their future-audit framing is a thought experiment, not a source of information. Nothing here accuses any person or institution of a crime.

## Where things stand

The review found that 53 of the 100 schemes merit investigative attention.

| Rating | Schemes | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Documented | 22 | A court, regulator or official record shows essentially this conduct |
| Plausible lead | 31 | The mechanism is real, but it is a crime only on specific facts, or the scale is unproven |
| Lawful or policy | 31 | The practice is real, but it is legal or a policy dispute |
| Unsupported | 16 | No supporting record was found, or the record contradicts the claim |

This is a first pass by an AI system, Claude, checked against public sources as of October 31, 2026. **No lawyer, bank examiner or financial-crime investigator has reviewed it yet.** That is what this repository is for.

## What was corrected

This is the corrected edition. Annex B of the petition lists 23 factual corrections and 4 formula errors, and all of them are applied here. Threshold tests now say "review" rather than "illegal" or "fraudulent," because a ratio cannot prove a crime.

Some problems remain:

- **Loaded language:** the "Future Verdict" labels still use words like "theft," "extortion" and "cartel." Those are the author's framing, not findings.
- **Unsupported schemes:** they stay in the documents so readers can see and test them. The tracker marks them.
- **Thresholds:** none has been back-tested against real data.
- **Gemini scripts:** the 19 embedded scripts are unrun prototypes. They send data to an outside model and trust its reply. Do not use them on real or restricted data.

The nine [original documents](archive/original/) are kept for comparison.

## Sources and limits

Claude checked about 50 case, statute and regulatory claims against public sources. Links appear beside each claim in the petition. Most are primary sources, though several rest on secondary reporting. Annex C of the petition gives the full AI use declaration.

Several cited cases are still open, so check their current status before relying on them. Pending matters are allegations, and everyone charged is presumed innocent.
