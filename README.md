# War Against Criminal Employees of Banks

**An open catalogue of 100 banking-crime hypotheses, published for public review.** Anyone can read the documents, check the claims, and challenge the ratings.

The catalogue backs an [open petition](#the-open-petition) asking the Nobel institutions to recognize the fight against crime in the banking industry. No Nobel Prize in Warfare exists, and the petition says so.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **These documents are leads, not evidence.** They describe theoretical schemes and name no suspect. Their future-audit framing is a thought experiment, not a source of information. Nothing here accuses any person or institution of a crime.

## The Open Petition

### MEMORANDUM & OPEN PETITION

**TO:** The Nobel Foundation (Stockholm) and the Norwegian Nobel Committee (Oslo)

**VIA:** Open Transmittal to Financial Law Enforcement, Bank Supervisors and the Global Anti-Financial-Crime Community

**FROM:** General 23 / Utah-1

**SUBJECT:** Proposal for the Inaugural Recognition of Warfare Against Crime in the Banking Industry

### I. The Strategic Imperative: The Ledger as a Battlespace

Crime in the banking industry is a war of attrition against treasuries, depositors and pensioners, and it is fought inside ledgers. Germany's Federal Court of Justice [ruled in 2021](https://www.hrr-strafrecht.de/hrr/1/20/1-519-20-1.pdf) that claiming refunds of tax never withheld through cum-ex trades is criminal tax evasion. Lehman Brothers moved [$50.38 billion](https://www.banking.senate.gov/download/040611valukas-testimony&download=1) off its balance sheet through Repo 105 at the close of the second quarter of 2008. TD Bank [pleaded guilty in 2024](https://occ.gov/news-issuances/news-releases/2024/nr-occ-2024-116.html) in a matter that cost about $3.09 billion across agencies.

These cases exploit four recurring weaknesses: the gap between trade and settlement, ledgers that never reconcile with one another, supervision by quarter-end snapshot, and collateral no outsider can check. An adversary who operates in those gaps can do lasting damage without firing a shot.

The defenders fight at a disadvantage, because the evidence arrives years late and the proof is hard. A US appeals court [reversed two LIBOR convictions](https://www.justice.gov/criminal-vns/case/connolly-black) in 2022, the UK Supreme Court [quashed two more](https://supremecourt.uk/cases/uksc-2024-0087) in 2025, and the Second Circuit [vacated an FX front-running conviction](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca2/24-1221/24-1221-2025-07-17.html) the same year. Recognizing valor on this front means honoring the people who turn suspicion into evidence that survives a courtroom.

### II. Operational Assets: The Nine-Part Investigative Arsenal

The nine *Future Disclosure Banking Crimes* documents form an open catalogue of investigative hypotheses, written as a retrospective thought experiment from a future audit vantage point. They describe theoretical offenders and name no suspect. Their value lies in the typologies and audit tests that hold up against the public record, as reviewed in [Annex A](petition/the-case-for-nobel-recognition.md#annex-a-plausibility-review-of-the-nine-documents).

The full catalogue is [open for public review on GitHub](https://github.com/utahisnotastate/ufwwaragainstcriminalemployeesofbanks). It holds the corrected documents, the originals, a record of every change, and a [scheme tracker](review/scheme-tracker.md) that gives all 100 schemes a stable ID, a rating and a one-line basis. Anyone can [challenge a claim, a rating or a formula](CONTRIBUTING.md) in public, using the evidence standards published there.

- **Field Directive 01 | Collateral, Settlement and Benchmark Integrity (Part 1)**
  - *Operational Scope:* Tests for double-pledged collateral, quarter-end window dressing, benchmark manipulation, off-market sovereign swaps and illicit flows through correspondent accounts.
  - *Public-Record Precedent:* Lehman's Repo 105, the [2015 FX antitrust guilty pleas](https://justice.gov/opa/pr/five-major-banks-agree-parent-level-guilty-pleas) by four banks, and [ADR pre-release cases](https://www.sec.gov/adr-enforcement) totaling nearly $432 million.
  - *Source:* [Future Disclosure Banking Crimes Part 1](documents/part-1.md).
- **Field Directive 02 | Synthetic Ownership and Client-Order Conflicts (Part 2)**
  - *Operational Scope:* Exposes concealed positions built through total return swaps, along with pre-hedging abuse, last-look abuse, Treasury auction misconduct and non-cash income inflation.
  - *Public-Record Precedent:* [Archegos](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/founder-and-head-archegos-capital-management-bill-hwang-sentenced-18-years-prison), which brought an 18-year sentence that was reported on appeal in 2026; [Barclays' $150 million last-look order](https://www.cfo.com/news/barclays-fined-150m-over-last-look-system/662877/); and [Salomon's $290 million auction settlement](https://www.justice.gov/archive/atr/public/press_releases/1992/211182.htm).
  - *Source:* [Future Disclosure Banking Crimes Part 2 (LEO Guide 2026)](documents/part-2.md).
- **Field Directive 03 | Capital, Valuation and Reserve Reporting (Part 3)**
  - *Operational Scope:* Targets internal-model compression, circular risk transfer, broker-quote marks, ETF settlement failures, layered fees and misstated stablecoin reserves.
  - *Public-Record Precedent:* The [London Whale mismarking case](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2013-187) ($200 million SEC penalty) and the [CFTC's Tether order](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/pressReleases/8450-21), which found sufficient fiat reserves on only 27.6% of sampled days.
  - *Source:* [Future Disclosure Banking Crimes Part 3 (LEO Guide, 2026)](documents/part-3.md).
- **Field Directive 04 | Physical Title and Bilateral Marks (Part 4)**
  - *Operational Scope:* Cross-checks warehouse receipts against lender records, compares both sides of bilateral derivative marks, and audits margin treatment across client tiers.
  - *Public-Record Precedent:* [Qingdao](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2018-12-12/dezheng-founder-gets-32-years-in-prison-over-port-forgery-scandal-101358154.html), where the main offender received 23 years, and the [First Brands indictment](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/first-brands-executives-charged-multibillion-dollar-fraud), which alleges double-pledged receivables. Two defendants there have pleaded not guilty and remain presumed innocent.
  - *Source:* [Future Disclosure Banking Crimes Part 4 (LEO Guide, 2026)](documents/part-4.md).
- **Field Directive 05 | Client Assets and Mortgage Servicing (Part 5)**
  - *Operational Scope:* Traces client collateral through offshore affiliates, tests segregation of omnibus accounts, and audits servicer fees charged against defaulted mortgages.
  - *Public-Record Precedent:* [MF Global](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/7095-14), where the CFTC charged unlawful use of nearly $1 billion in customer funds, and the [FTX conviction](https://ww3.ca2.uscourts.gov/decisions/OPN/24-961_opn.pdf), affirmed in June 2026.
  - *Source:* [Future Disclosure Banking Crimes Part 5 (LEO Guide) 2026](documents/part-5.md).
- **Field Directive 06 | Hidden Credit Deterioration (Part 6)**
  - *Operational Scope:* Flags amend-and-extend loan concealment, optimistic recovery inputs and unsupported loan classifications in private credit and structured vehicles.
  - *Public-Record Precedent:* [Tricolor](https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/ceo-cfo-coo-charged-connection-billion-dollar-collapse-tricolor-auto), where prosecutors allege about $2.2 billion of collateral was pledged against about $1.4 billion that was real. Three former executives have pleaded guilty; the remaining defendant has pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent.
  - *Source:* [Future Disclosure Banking Crimes Part 6 (LEO Guide) 2026](documents/part-6.md).
- **Field Directive 07 | Public-Entity Swaps and Cargo Finance (Part 7)**
  - *Operational Scope:* Reviews municipal derivative dealings, duplicate bills of lading, custody of dividend cash in transit and tokenized collateral.
  - *Public-Record Precedent:* [Jefferson County, Alabama](https://www.sec.gov/news/press/2009/2009-232.htm), a $722 million SEC settlement over undisclosed payments to win bond and swap business, and [Hin Leong](https://www.singaporelawwatch.sg/Headlines/hin-leong-founder-ok-lims-jail-term-cut-to-135-years-after-appeal-to-high-court), where the founder's cheating and forgery conviction was upheld in 2026.
  - *Source:* [Future Disclosure Banking Crimes Part 7 (LEO Guide) 2026](documents/part-7.md).
- **Field Directive 08 | Foreclosure Standing, Consumer Harm and Spoofing (Part 8)**
  - *Operational Scope:* Tests mortgage assignment chains for forged or backdated documents, reviews loan-servicing payment allocation, and detects spoofing.
  - *Public-Record Precedent:* The [$25 billion National Mortgage Settlement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/2012/March/12-asg-306.html), [Navient's $120 million CFPB settlement](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/enforcement/actions/navient-corporation-navient-solutions-inc-and-pioneer-credit-recovery-inc/) and [JPMorgan's $920 million spoofing resolution](https://www.justice.gov/criminal/criminal-vns/case/jpmorgan-chase-co-deferred-prosecution-agreement).
  - *Source:* [Future Disclosure Banking Crimes Part 8 (LEO Guide, 2026)](documents/part-8.md).
- **Field Directive 09 | Green Claims, Insider Hedges and Retail Mis-selling (Part 9)**
  - *Operational Scope:* Audits green-proceeds use and carbon-credit duplication, insider hedging disclosures, trade-invoice fraud, wash trades and the sale of bail-in debt to retail clients.
  - *Public-Record Precedent:* The [DWS greenwashing penalties](https://www.ipe.com/news/frankfurt-public-prosecutor-fines-dws-after-greenwashing-investigation/10129803.article), the [CQC carbon-credit prosecution](https://justice.gov/usao-sdny/pr/us-attorney-announces-criminal-charges-multi-year-fraud-scheme-market-carbon-credits) (still unproven against the remaining defendants), and the [UK's retail CoCo restrictions](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps15-14-restrictions-retail-distribution-regulatory-capital).
  - *Source:* [Future Disclosure Banking Crimes Part 9 (LEO Guide, 2026)](documents/part-9.md).

### III. Rules of Engagement: From Lead to Evidence

The catalogue is a source of leads, never of proof, and 53 of its 100 schemes merit investigative attention. Of those, 22 match documented conduct and 31 are plausible leads. Another 31 describe lawful conduct or policy disputes, and 16 lack support. These ratings are a first pass by an AI system. No lawyer, bank examiner or financial-crime investigator has reviewed them yet, which is why the work is open to public challenge.

1. **Start from a lawful predicate.** No investigation should open on the catalogue alone. A lead needs independent facts tied to a specific subject.
2. **Prove the offense, not the pattern.** Each case must establish the statutory elements, including intent where required. Reusing collateral with consent is lawful; pledging the same asset twice as unencumbered is the fraud.
3. **Treat every threshold as a screen.** The documents' ratios have not been back-tested. A flag justifies a closer look and establishes nothing about guilt.
4. **Keep AI output out of the verdict.** The Gemini scripts are unrun prototypes fed simulated data. Their "fraud" scores are model opinions, not evidence, and [FRE 702](https://www.law.cornell.edu/rules/fre/rule_702) requires the proponent to show reliability.
5. **Protect restricted data.** [Suspicious activity report confidentiality](https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/31/1020.320) has no general exception for third-party services, so SAR material must not enter a commercial AI pipeline without legal clearance.
6. **Preserve the presumption of innocence.** The documents name no suspect and this petition accuses no one. Pending matters are cited as allegations.

### IV. The Case for the Nobel Recognition in Warfare

No Nobel Prize in Warfare exists, and the Nobel Foundation's [own history](https://www.nobelprize.org/were-in-this-forever-the-history-of-the-nobel-foundation/) records its answer that no new prizes would be created. This petition asks the Foundation to hear the case anyway, because the Nobel tradition honors the defense of human welfare. Yet peace without an honest ledger is an illusion.

A nation cannot defend itself if its treasury pays refunds for tax never collected, its banks report capital they do not have, or its pensioners hold claims on collateral already pledged elsewhere. The forensic accountants, examiners, prosecutors and whistleblowers who expose these acts serve on an active front. So do those who build open investigative methods for them.

This petition makes four requests:

1. **To the Nobel Foundation and the Norwegian Nobel Committee:** consider recognition of frontline valor in Financial Integrity and Banking Crime Defense. If no new category is possible, consider this work within the Peace Prize's concern for the foundations of peace.
2. **To [qualified nominators](https://www.nobelpeaceprize.org/nobel-peace-prize/nomination/criteria-for-nominators):** weigh nominating individuals whose work has produced proven results. Self-nomination is not permitted, so this memorandum cannot itself serve as a nomination.
3. **To financial law enforcement and bank supervisors:** test the corrected catalogue against closed cases, publish what works, and discard what does not.
4. **To lawyers, examiners, investigators and the public:** [review the catalogue](CONTRIBUTING.md), challenge any claim with evidence, and help separate proven conduct from speculation.

The measure of this arsenal should be results: cases opened, frauds proven, and losses recovered. National defense begins in the ledger, and this petition calls upon the law enforcement community to test these tools and the Nobel assembly to recognize frontline valor on this front.

## What was corrected

This is the corrected edition. Annex B of the petition lists 23 factual corrections and 4 formula errors, and all of them are applied here. Threshold tests now say "review" rather than "illegal" or "fraudulent," because a ratio cannot prove a crime.

Some problems remain:

- **Loaded language:** the "Future Verdict" labels still use words like "theft," "extortion" and "cartel." Those are the author's framing, not findings.
- **Unsupported schemes:** they stay in the documents so readers can see and test them. The tracker marks them.
- **Thresholds:** none has been back-tested against real data.
- **Gemini scripts:** the 19 embedded scripts are unrun prototypes. They send data to an outside model and trust its reply. Do not use them on real or restricted data.

The nine [original documents](archive/original/) are kept for comparison.
