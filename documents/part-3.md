### 5. Immediate Forensic Verification Protocol

To verify whether institutional counterparties are actively deploying ETF operational shorting or leveraged LDI margin structures, execute the following audit checklist:

**Calculate the ETF Operational Shorting Index (EOSI):**

```math
\text{EOSI}=\frac{\text{Aggregate Unsettled Creation Basket Fails-to-Deliver}}{\text{Total ETF Shares Outstanding}}
```

If EOSI > 0.03 (3%) for more than 6 consecutive settlement cycles while the authorized participant maintains active net-short derivatives on the underlying constituent basket, review the desk's Reg SHO compliance and the basis for any market-making exception.

**Calculate the Sovereign LDI Fragility Coefficient (SLFC):**

```math
\text{SLFC}=\frac{\text{Leveraged Swap Notional} \times \Delta y_{\text{stress}}}{\text{Unencumbered Liquid Cash Buffers}}
```

Where Δy<sub>stress</sub> represents a 100bps rate movement. If SLFC > 1.0, the vehicle may face forced collateral sales in a yield shock. Correction: multiply swap notional by duration as well as the rate move. The formula above omits duration and understates the margin call.

**Audit Multi-Tier Private Asset Fee Compounding:** Review private placement memorandums (PPMs) for public pension allocations. Sum the cumulative gross-to-net fee drag across all master, feeder, and affiliated co-investment SPVs. If the cumulative fee drag exceeds 350bps (3.5%) annualized against gross yield, review fee disclosures and conflicts of interest.
