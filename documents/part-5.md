### 5. Immediate Forensic Verification Protocol

To verify whether institutional trading conglomerates or prime brokerage desks are actively deploying cross-border rehypothecation evasion or derivative netting illusions, execute the following audit checklist:

**Calculate the Cross-Border Rehypothecation Velocity (CBRV):**

```math
\text{CBRV}=\frac{\text{Total Collateral Pledged by Offshore Affiliates}}{\text{Originating Client Domestic Debit Balance}}
```

If CBRV > 1.40 (140%), review where the client collateral sits and whether the client consented.

**Compute the Gross-to-Net Derivative Distortion Ratio (GNDR):**

```math
\text{GNDR}=\frac{\text{Gross Total Notional Derivatives}}{\text{Reported Net Balance-Sheet Derivative Exposure}}
```

If GNDR > 15.0 and gross notional exposure exceeds 50× the institution's Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) capital, note that this ratio is high at every large dealer by design. Notional is not exposure, so review netting-set enforceability instead.

**Calculate the Index Rebalance Slippage Drag (IRSD):**

```math
\text{IRSD}=\frac{P_{\text{MOC Closing Cross}}-P_{T-15\text{Days}}}{P_{T-15\text{Days}}}
```

For all new additions to major passive indices, calculate the price divergence between the date of first inclusion announcement and the official rebalance date. The S&amp;P 500 addition effect has fallen below 1% in the past decade, and trading on public index announcements is not front-running, so this test alone shows no offense.

### 5. Immediate Forensic Verification Protocol

To verify whether institutional clearinghouses or broker-dealers are actively using bilateral FTD warehousing or run-throttling asymmetries, execute the following audit checklist:

**Calculate the Ex-Clearing Settlement Latency Skew (ESLS):**

```math
\text{ESLS}=\frac{\Delta t_{\text{Retail}}}{\Delta t_{\text{Prime}}}
```

If ESLS > 100.0 during elevated credit spread sessions while gross deposits decline, review the cause. Retail batch rails are slower than wholesale wires in normal conditions, so a skew alone proves nothing.

**Compute the Bilateral FTD Ratio (BFTR):**

```math
\text{BFTR}=\frac{\text{Bilateral Ex-Clearing Unsettled FTDs}}{\text{Reported Exchange Continuous Net Settlement FTDs}}
```

If BFTR > 2.0, review ex-clearing fails, which SEC fails-to-deliver data does not appear to capture.

**Audit Omnibus Yield Siphoning Margin:** Examine bank disclosures regarding sweep accounts and compare the gross repo yields generated by the depository against the annualized interest remitted to underlying depositors. A net interest capture exceeding 400bps warrants review of sweep disclosures.
