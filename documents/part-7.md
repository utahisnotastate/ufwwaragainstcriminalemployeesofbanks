### 5. Immediate Forensic Verification Protocol

To verify whether public pension managers, municipal authorities, or collateral desks are actively deploying actuarial smoothing or tokenized rehypothecation in active portfolios, execute the following audit checklist:

**Calculate the Pension Actuarial Liability Deflation Index (PALDI):**

```math
\text{PALDI}=\left(\frac{r_{\text{assumed}}-r_{\text{risk-free}}}{r_{\text{risk-free}}}\right) \times \text{Duration} \times \text{Reported Liabilities}
```

Where Duration averages 14years. If r(assumed) - r(risk-free) > 2.50% (250bps), review the discount rate. Correction: estimate the understatement as duration × rate gap × reported liabilities. The formula above wrongly divides by the risk-free rate. At 14 years and a 2.5-point gap, the estimate is about 35% of liabilities.

**Compute the Municipal Termination Fee Burden Ratio (MTBR):**

```math
\text{MTBR}=\frac{\text{Swap Early Termination Penalty}}{\text{Annual Operating Civic Revenue}}
```

If MTBR > 0.50 (50%), review the swap's advice, pricing and conflicts.

**Verify Tokenized Collateral Custodial Segregation:** Cross-reference on-chain tokenized Treasury smart contracts against Depository Trust Company (DTC) participant accounts. If the custodian bank reports active bilateral repo borrowings secured by the identical CUSIPs referenced by the on-chain mint contract, flag the asset for review of possible double-pledging.
