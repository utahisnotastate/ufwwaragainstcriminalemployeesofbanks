### 5. Immediate Forensic Verification Protocol

To determine whether institutional mortgage originators, distressed debt syndicates, or central-bank interfaces are actively deploying title decoupling or collateral-stripping mechanisms, execute the following audit checklist:

**Calculate the Title Severance Latency Delta (TSLD):**

```math
\text{TSLD}=t_{\text{Allonge Assignment Execution}}-t_{\text{Securitization Trust Closing Date}}
```

If TSLD > 90days and is dated after the borrower's default, inspect the assignment for forgery or backdating under the trust's pooling and servicing agreement (PSA). Late dating alone is not proof.

**Compute the Collateral Stripping Subordination Ratio (CSSR):**

```math
\text{CSSR}=\frac{\text{Valuation of Assets Transferred to Unrestricted Subsidiaries}}{\text{Total Collateral Pledged to Existing Senior Secured Debt}}
```

If CSSR > 0.25 (25%) and new priority liens are placed on the transferred assets without 100% existing creditor consent, review the credit agreement and any fraudulent-transfer claim. Such deals often turn on contract terms.

**Audit Unreported Sovereign Net-to-Gross FX Reserve Depletion:** Cross-match central bank reported gross foreign-exchange assets against confidential Bank for International Settlements (BIS) and regional cross-currency repo liabilities. If net unencumbered reserves fall below 30% of reported gross figures, review the reserve position. This gap alone does not show concealment.
