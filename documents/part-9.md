### 5. Immediate Forensic Verification Protocol

To determine whether institutional dealers or private equity cartels are actively deploying haircut creep monetization or dual-rate currency flight in active datasets, execute the following audit checklist:

**Calculate the Central Bank Collateral Dislocation Index (CBCDI):**

```math
\text{CBCDI}=\frac{\text{Central Bank Advanced Cash Liquidity}}{\text{Audited Arm's-Length Private Secondary Market Bid}}
```

If CBCDI > 1.50 (150%) on any non-sovereign asset pledged to an emergency facility, review the facility's terms and valuation.

**Compute the Trade-Invoicing Forex Extraction Ratio (TIFER):**

```math
\text{TIFER}=\frac{\text{Declared Import Invoiced Dollar Allocation}}{\text{Empirical Physical Freight Par Value (IoT/Customs Sensor Record)}}
```

If TIFER > 2.0 while the host nation operates official capital controls, review for trade-based money laundering.

**Audit Intercompany LBO Interest Coverage Drag (ILICD):**

```math
\text{ILICD}=\frac{\text{Annual Intercompany Debt Interest Remitted Offshore}}{\text{Domestic Operating EBITDA}}
```

If ILICD > 0.90 (90%) for a business acquired via leveraged buyout while corporate income tax is zeroed out, review interest-limitation and transfer-pricing compliance.
