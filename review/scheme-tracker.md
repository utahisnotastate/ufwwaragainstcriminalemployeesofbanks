# Scheme Tracker

Every scheme in the catalogue has a stable ID, a rating and a one-line basis, so you can challenge any single call.

Ratings are review judgments as of October 31, 2026, not legal findings. No lawyer, examiner or financial-crime investigator has reviewed them yet. "Annex B" means the correction is listed in the [petition](../petition/the-case-for-nobel-recognition.md#annex-b-corrections-before-law-enforcement-use).

To dispute a rating, [open a rating challenge](https://github.com/utahisnotastate/ufwwaragainstcriminalemployeesofbanks/issues/new?template=rating-challenge.yml) and quote the scheme ID.
